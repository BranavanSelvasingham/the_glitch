# Target-Age Playtest Protocol

The acceptance target is at least 8 target-age players, with guardian consent,
across at least 30 ten-minute sessions. Do not store names, contact details,
photos, voice, or video in this repository.

## Setup

- Use anonymous codes such as `P01` and session codes such as `P01-S1`.
- Confirm guardian consent outside the repository; record only `yes` or `no`.
- Use the physical iPad at comfortable brightness and about 50% speaker volume.
- Start with sound on. Use a fresh development-install state only if deleting
  existing app data has been explicitly approved.
- Seat the observer beside, not in front of, the player. Do not coach the first
  run or explain what screen elements mean.

## Ten-minute session

1. Hand over the title screen and say only: **“You can try this game.”**
2. Record whether the player starts unaided and understands hold/release within
   15 seconds.
3. On the first run, record 20-second survival, visible confusion, each death's
   likely cause, and whether the player restarts without prompting.
4. After a death, ask neutrally: **“What happened?”** and **“Did that feel fair
   or unfair?”** Do not suggest an answer.
5. After five minutes, ask:
   - “What is Aidan trying to do?”
   - “Who or what is The Glitch?”
   - “Which worlds do you remember?”
   - “Do these worlds feel more like nature/fantasy or technology/cyber?”
6. Ask for 1–5 ratings for appearance, animation, sound, controls, fairness, and
   fun. Explain that 1 means poor and 5 means excellent.
7. At ten minutes, record total runs, voluntary restarts, frustration quit, and
   a short no-PII observation note.

## North-star result per session

A session passes the five-minute north star only when all are true:

- Controls are understood without help.
- At least two worlds are recognized.
- Aidan's goal and The Glitch's role are both recalled.
- Appearance and fun are each rated at least 4/5.
- Another run starts without prompting.

## Aggregate release targets

- North-star pass: ≥80% of sessions.
- Controls understood within 15 seconds: ≥90%.
- First-run 20-second survival: ≥80%.
- Understood/fair deaths: ≥90% of observed deaths.
- Appearance ≥4.3/5; animation and sound ≥4.2/5; controls ≥4.3/5;
  fun ≥4.2/5.
- Voluntary restart: ≥70% of players and ≥75% wanting another run.
- Median at least 3 runs per ten minutes; frustration quits ≤10%.
- Story/role recall ≥80%; all-world distinction ≥90%; fantasy/nature wording
  ≥80%.

Record every session, including weak results. Do not average away a hard failure
or exclude a player because the game performed poorly.
